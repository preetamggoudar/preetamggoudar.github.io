01  /  About

Building at the intersection of AI, web and people.

I'm Preetam Goudar, a Computer Science student specialising in Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning at Shri Siddhartha Institute of Technology, in collaboration with upGrad School of Technology (Batch 2026–2030).

I'm most interested in turning model experiments into things people can actually use — from vision-based extraction systems to AI web apps. I learn fastest by shipping, and I care just as much about the people around the technology as the technology itself.

As a Google Student Ambassador 2026, I help students explore Gemini and Google's AI ecosystem through peer-learning sessions and hands-on events. Away from the screen, I play guitar and spend time with the hackathon community.
